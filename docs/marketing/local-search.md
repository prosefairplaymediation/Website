## 2. Reviews — you have 28 at 5.0; protect the velocity

Review count and velocity are among the strongest local ranking factors, and the
deciding factor for a stranger choosing between two mediators. At 28 with a
perfect 5.0 you are well past the threshold where reviews look thin, so the goal
shifts from *accumulating* to **not going quiet**. A profile with 28 reviews and
nothing in eight months reads worse than one with 15 and a new one last week.

Target a steady trickle — two or three a month beats a burst.

### Your one-tap review link

```
https://g.page/r/CQBgcXw1OG36EAE/review
```

Use this one, never the Maps link. It opens the review box directly. The Maps
link makes people hunt for the button, and you lose most of them at that step.

**Test it once in a private window** before you start sending it.

### When to ask

**Immediately after the agreement is signed**, while the relief is fresh. Not a
week later, not batched at month end. The gap between "this is finally over" and
your ask is the single biggest variable in whether you get a review at all.

### Two things that make reviews work harder

**Ask them to mention the service and the place, in their own words** — "mediated
our parenting plan, Boca Raton". Review text is indexed, and it is one of the few
places location and service keywords appear without you writing them yourself.
Never script it for them; just say what was most useful is worth mentioning.

**Never coach the content.** Beyond that nudge, let them write what they write.

### Three rules you cannot break

1. **Never incentivize a review.** No discounts, no gift cards. Google removes
   them and can penalize the profile.
2. **Never review-gate** — screening for happy clients first and only asking
   those. It violates Google's policy.
3. **⚠️ Never confirm case details in a review response.** This is the § 44.405
   trap and it is easy to walk into. A client may choose to disclose that they
   mediated with you; *you* still cannot. So responses stay generic:

   > ✅ "Thank you — I'm glad the process worked for you."
   > ❌ "So glad we got the parenting plan resolved!"

   The second confirms a mediation communication. Keep every response to one
   warm, contentless sentence.

**Respond to all of them,** including any negative one, briefly and without
defensiveness. Response rate is visible to both Google and to readers.

## 5. Measurement

- **Google Search Console** — verify the domain, submit `sitemap.xml`. This is
  the only place you see real query data. Non-negotiable.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** — same, and it matters because of the ChatGPT link.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** — free, cookieless, so no consent banner. The
  right choice for a family-law site where visitors are already anxious about
  privacy. Avoid anything that requires a cookie notice.
- **GBP Insights** — track calls, booking clicks, and direction requests monthly.

Log all of it alongside the AI prompt set in `tracking/`.

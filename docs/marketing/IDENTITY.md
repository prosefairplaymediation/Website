## Facts I verified and used throughout

These are Florida-accurate as of research on 2026-08-28. Re-check the fee
figures annually — they're set by the Legislature and drift.

- Residency: one party must have resided in Florida **6 months** before filing
  (Fla. Stat. § 61.021).
- Filing fee: **~$408** for dissolution, **+$10** for issuance of summons.
  Varies slightly by county.
- Simplified dissolution eligibility: **no minor children, no pregnancy, no
  alimony sought, full agreement on property.** Finalizes in roughly 30 days.
- Private mediator rates statewide: **$200–$350/hour.**
- Court-connected mediation: **$60–$120 per person per session** where combined
  household income is under $100,000; **free** for parties determined indigent.
- Indigent status waives the *filing fee* — not service of process, and not
  mediation fees.
- Rule 12.740: contested family matters may be referred to mediation; mediation
  must be completed within **75 days** of the first conference.
- Confidentiality: mediation communications are confidential and privileged
  under **Fla. Stat. § 44.405**, with narrow exceptions (written waiver by all
  parties, planning/committing a crime, mandatory child or elder abuse
  reporting, and claims of mediator misconduct or malpractice).
- A mandatory **Parenting Plan** governs shared parental responsibility;
  courts weigh the statutory best-interest factors under Chapter 61.

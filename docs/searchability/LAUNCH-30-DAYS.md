# First 30 days

One thing per day. Nothing here takes more than 20 minutes except where noted.

## Before day 1 — two blockers (30 min, must be done first)

- [ ] Fill in `IDENTITY.md`, including **`{{CERT_BODY}}`** — the organization that
      issued your certification
- [ ] Read `compliance/florida-mediator-rules.md` in full. Particularly § 44.405:
      **no real case details, ever, on any platform.** This is the one mistake in
      this program with consequences beyond marketing.

## Week 1 — establish the accounts, post nothing professional

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Create the Reddit account. Human-sounding username, **not** the business name. Verify email, add avatar and one-line bio. |
| 2 | Join the Tier 1 subs from `reddit/02-subreddit-map.md`. Read each one's rules page and wiki. Note any that prohibit professional participation. |
| 3 | Create the Quora account. Set the credential line and bio from `quora/01-profile-setup.md`. Add all topic authorities. |
| 4 | Reddit: 10 low-stakes comments in non-professional subs. Your city, a hobby, r/AskReddit. One sentence each is fine. |
| 5 | Same. 10 more comments. Target: 100+ comment karma by end of week 1. |
| 6 | Run the `site:reddit.com` searches from `reddit/02-subreddit-map.md`. Build your back-catalog target list in the tracking log. Do not comment yet. |
| 7 | Search all the Quora target questions from `quora/02-target-questions.md`. Shortlist the 10 with the highest views and weakest existing answers. |

## What day 30 looks like

Roughly 12 published answers, an established account on each platform, mod
relationships opened, and a baseline measurement.

**You will almost certainly see no citations and little traffic.** That's the
expected result and not a sign of failure — see the month-by-month table in
`reddit/04-cadence.md`. The citation payoff lands around month 3, and the answers
published in week 2 are the ones that will still be earning in year three.

The one thing that would make this month wasted: getting the account filtered in
week 1 by skipping the warm-up. Everything else is recoverable.

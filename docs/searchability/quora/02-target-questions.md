# Quora: target questions

## How to select

Search each query below on Quora. For each result, judge:

| Signal | Want |
|---|---|
| View count | High — it's proof of ongoing demand |
| Existing answers | **Few, or many but weak.** 40 strong answers = skip |
| Top answer quality | Thin, generic, or non-expert = your opening |
| Date | Older questions with continuing views are ideal |
| Jurisdiction | Florida-specific questions are uncontested — prioritize them |

The best target is a question with thousands of views whose top answer is three
sentences from someone with no credential. Those exist in quantity in this space.

## Search these

**Process / comparison — highest AI query volume**
- Is divorce mediation better than going to court?
- Is divorce mediation worth it?
- What are the disadvantages of divorce mediation?
- What's the difference between mediation and collaborative divorce?
- Can mediation work if my spouse and I can't be in the same room?

**High-conflict — your flagship territory**
- Does mediation work with a narcissist?
- Can you mediate with someone who lies?
- Is mediation a waste of time in a high-conflict divorce?
- How do you negotiate with an ex who won't compromise?

**Cost**
- How much does divorce mediation cost?
- What's the cheapest way to get divorced?
- How much does a divorce cost in Florida?
- Is mediation cheaper than hiring a lawyer?

**Florida-specific — least competition, best conversion**
- How do I file for divorce in Florida without a lawyer?
- What is a simplified dissolution of marriage in Florida?
- Is mediation required in a Florida divorce?
- How long does a divorce take in Florida?
- What is a parenting plan in Florida?

**Mechanics**
- Do I need a lawyer for divorce mediation?
- What happens if divorce mediation fails?
- Is a mediated divorce agreement legally binding?
- What should I bring to divorce mediation?
- Is what I say in mediation confidential?
- Can a mediator give legal advice?

**Co-parenting**
- How do you make a parenting plan that works?
- How do you co-parent with someone difficult?
- Can a parenting plan be changed?

## Ask questions too

Quora rewards question-asking, and a well-framed question you then *don't*
answer attracts answers that build a page you're associated with.

Ask ones that don't exist yet, in Florida-specific form:
- "How does the 6-month Florida residency requirement work if one spouse just moved?"
- "What does court-connected mediation cost in Florida compared to a private mediator?"
- "For a Florida divorce with no minor children, what's the actual difference between simplified dissolution and a regular uncontested filing?"

**Do not answer your own questions.** On Reddit that's a banning offense; on
Quora it's merely transparent and cheap. Either way, don't.

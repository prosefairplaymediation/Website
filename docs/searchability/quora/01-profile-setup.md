# Quora: profile setup

Quora is a smaller citation source than Reddit but it has one property Reddit
doesn't: **it attributes you by name.** Reddit cites a thread; Quora cites an
answer attached to a credentialed human. For building *you* as the recognized
entity, Quora punches above its citation share.

## The single highest-leverage string in this entire program

Your **credential line** — the text under your name on every answer.

This is what gets scraped alongside your answer text, and it's how an AI engine
learns that a named person with a specific specialty said this. Get it right
once and it propagates across every answer you ever write.

Set it to:

> **Certified mediator, advanced certification in high-conflict resolution | Florida**

Notes on why it's built this way:
- **Specialty before geography.** The specialty is what makes you distinctive;
  Florida is what makes you findable. Both matter, specialty ranks.
- **"High-conflict resolution" is the differentiating token.** Almost nobody
  else in this space has it in their credential line.
- Add `{{CERT_BODY}}` if it fits the character limit — an attributed
  certification is stronger than a bare one.
- Do **not** put a URL in the credential line. Quora down-ranks promotional
  profiles, and the line is for authority, not traffic.

## Profile bio

Quora indexes this and AI engines read it. Write it as third-person-adjacent
plain prose, no marketing voice:

> Certified mediator with advanced certification in high-conflict resolution,
> practicing in Florida. Works primarily with couples handling their own divorce
> — self-represented and pro se cases, usually before anything is filed. Answers
> questions here about how Florida divorce and family mediation actually works:
> costs, parenting plans, what mediation can and can't do. Not legal advice —
> mediators can't give it.

That last sentence does real work. It's honest, it satisfies your ethical
obligation up front, and it reads as trustworthy to both humans and models.

## Topic authority

Quora routes questions by topic. Add yourself as knowledgeable in:

- Divorce Mediation
- Mediation
- Divorce
- Family Law
- Child Custody
- Co-Parenting
- Conflict Resolution
- Divorce in Florida (and any Florida-specific topics that exist)
- Alternative Dispute Resolution

Follow these topics too — that's how relevant questions reach your feed.

## Spaces

Join and answer in divorce, co-parenting, and family-law Spaces. Space answers
get additional distribution. Consider creating one — *"Divorce Without Court"* or
similar — after you have 20+ answers published. Not before; an empty Space
signals abandonment.

## What not to do

- **No links for the first ~20 answers.** Quora's spam classifier weights link
  ratio heavily on new accounts, and a demoted profile is very hard to recover.
  After that, at most one link per several answers, and only where genuinely
  responsive.
- **No short answers.** Quora suppresses thin content. 250 words is a floor;
  400–700 is the range that ranks.
- **Don't answer questions with 40 existing answers.** You'll never surface.
  Target questions with high view counts and few or weak answers — see
  `02-target-questions.md`.
- **Never paste your Reddit text.** Duplicate content across platforms
  competes with itself and dilutes both. The Quora library is written from
  different angles on purpose.

## Cadence

Quora rewards **consistency over intensity** — the published pilot data showing
real results ran at roughly 4–6 hours per week. Two or three good answers a week,
sustained, beats twenty in one weekend followed by silence. The algorithm reads
abandonment.
